Security

Your data is encrypted with your password. It can't be recovered if you forget the password, so keep a JSON backup (above) somewhere safe.

How it works

Each time you train, tap the day, pick how long you've got (30 / 60 / 90 min), and choose the blocks you want. Your weights focus rotates Push → Pull → Legs, and cardio and flexibility rotate too — but only when you actually log a session. Miss a day and nothing shifts; you always pick up the next focus.

Starting out safely

You're beginning a new routine. Build up gradually — a couple of sessions a week is a real win — and let rest days do their job. If you're new to intense exercise, it's worth a quick chat with your GP before ramping up. This app is a planner, not medical advice.
